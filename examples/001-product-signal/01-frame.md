# Frame: Product Signal

## Problem

Users want to say "I care about this product" but the product currently has no first-class signal. The team only sees scattered comments and private messages.

## Current Alternative

Users send messages manually or say nothing. The team infers demand from noisy channels.

## Baseline

The product detail page can explain the item, but it cannot capture demand.

## Desired Outcome

Users can leave a simple signal, and the team can see aggregate interest per product.

## Success Criteria

- A signed-in user can add and remove one interest signal per product.
- Product detail shows an aggregate count.
- Individual users are not publicly listed.

## Appetite

Human appetite:
- 1 week MVP

Codex execution budget:
- One product detail surface
- Local persistence or existing app persistence
- No marketplace, offers, direct messaging, or production auth changes
